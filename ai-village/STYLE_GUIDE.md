# My AI Village — Style Guide

Use this paragraph **word for word** in every art prompt, so all artwork matches.

> Modern HD-2D art for a cozy medieval village RPG, seen in a top-down three-quarter view. Detailed sprite-style buildings, furniture, props and trees set in a 3D diorama, with realistic soft lighting, ambient occlusion, gentle bloom on sunlit surfaces and a soft depth-of-field blur toward the top and bottom edges. A warm color palette of honey yellows, terracotta, warm browns, soft greens and golden light. Light comes from the upper left, so highlights sit on top-left edges and shadows fall to the lower right. Crisp, readable shapes with subtle dark outlines. High detail in wood grain, stone, roof tiles, fabric and foliage, with the same scale and perspective across every asset. No text anywhere: no letters, numbers, writing on signs, logos, watermarks or interface elements.
