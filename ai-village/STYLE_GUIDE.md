# My AI Village — Style Guide

Use this paragraph **word for word** in every art prompt, so all artwork matches.

> 16-bit SNES-era pixel art of a cozy village, drawn in a top-down three-quarter view like a classic console RPG. Crisp, clean pixels on a visible pixel grid, with no blur, soft gradients or anti-aliasing. A limited, warm color palette of honey yellows, terracotta, warm browns, soft greens and golden light. Light comes from the upper left, so highlights sit on top-left edges and shadows fall to the lower right. Every object has a dark outline that keeps its shape readable. High detail in wood grain, stone, roof tiles and foliage, with the same scale and perspective across every asset. No text anywhere: no letters, numbers, writing on signs, logos, watermarks or interface elements.
